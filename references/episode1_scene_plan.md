# মায়ের অবসর: original scene plan (condensed; later retimed and extended)

Story: a father leaves on a rainy night without leaving an umbrella. The mother raises Nusrat, Rahat and Mim by husking rice, working in other homes and sewing at night. She sells her bangles for Nusrat's college form, the children study through hardship and succeed (bank, engineer, medical college). On a Friday they return, Rahat swaps her needle for the key of a new pucca house; with a solid roof, the mother sleeps through the rain for the first time.

Cast: Fatema (mother), Nusrat (eldest daughter), Rahat (son), Mim (youngest), father as a silhouette. Costumes in `house_style_reference.md` section 4.
